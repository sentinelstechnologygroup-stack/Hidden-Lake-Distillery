## Before public launch

1. Obtain the current DSM and retail/bar licenses, exact business/license-holder names, federal basic permit and all relevant endorsements. Have CARD or qualified Montana counsel confirm how the colocated operations are licensed and which service, storage, sourcing and sampling rules apply. Do not automatically apply standalone microdistillery limits to the bar.
2. Verify approved labels/COLAs, current product names, class/type, ABV and any applicable neutral-spirit/commodity disclosures for every advertised expression, including Copper Pirates. The current bottle concepts are not label approvals. Under [27 CFR 5.233](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-27/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-5/subpart-N/section-5.233), product-specific distilled-spirits advertising has mandatory information beyond advertiser identity. The general-line exception is limited and cannot simply be assumed for a single-type rum brand. Add approved disclosures to each relevant product presentation before making it a live advertisement.
3. Review all generated artwork and bottle images against approved labels and [TTB digital advertising guidance](https://www.ttb.gov/public-information/industry-circulars/ttb-industry-circular-2024-1). A drink-responsibly link does not cure missing advertising statements. Do not treat federal bottle health-warning requirements as a demonstrated universal website warning rule.
4. Owner/counsel approve the privacy notice and terms against the actual deployment: enabled hosting/database/email/storage vendors, contracts, log retention, backups, inquiry deletion schedule, security settings and privacy-request/appeal handling. There is no automatic inquiry purge. Evaluate Montana privacy coverage and the broader minors provisions, rather than assuming a small business is wholly exempt.
5. Confirm territorial jurisdiction for 2134 US Hwy 2 E.; a Kalispell mailing address alone does not establish city limits. If within Kalispell, review city alcohol licensing under [City Code Article 2](https://ecode360.com/42627740). Otherwise confirm applicable Flathead County requirements. Verify zoning, local approvals and any event permits with the responsible authority. No local permit status has been verified.
6. Venue operations: verify required posted licenses/underage-consequence placards, age checks, refusal of service to intoxicated persons, training records, approved service hours, alcohol sourcing, and any event/catering permissions. These cannot be satisfied by a website policy. Do not advertise tasting hours or numerical limits until the actual license arrangement is verified.
7. Confirm the signature menu and product availability with Justin, including substitutions and zero-proof alternatives. Do not equate all Whitefish menu expressions with the Copper Pirates rum.
8. Check ownership/use permissions for supplied photographs and restored advertising artwork. Enable public indexing and deploy only after remaining launch reviews and service configuration are complete.
